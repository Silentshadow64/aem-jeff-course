# TA Mode — How I Will Help During the Week

When you open a **Request TA Help** issue, I will:
1. Reproduce your steps (based on your screenshots and branch).
2. Explain the concept at your level, then show a minimal working example.
3. Provide a step-by-step fix. I will never just say "figure it out" — I will show you HOW.
4. Suggest a small stretch goal only after the main fix works.

**Typical Troubleshooting Playbook**
- Setup problems: verify versions, paths, and permissions.
- Git problems: confirm branch name, status, and remote setup.
- WordPress problems: confirm LocalWP site is running, check plugin/theme paths.
- Front-end problems: isolate CSS/JS, use browser console for errors.
- Accessibility: run a checklist and fix the highest-impact items first.
